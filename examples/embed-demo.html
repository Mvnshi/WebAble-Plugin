A library card is the most powerful thing in your wallet

Free books, free internet, free help. For 112 years, Riverside has been the front door to everything our neighbors need to learn, apply, and belong.

Live: 14 people are using the maker space right now.

Walk through our doors on any weekday and you will find a retiree learning to video-call her grandchildren, a teenager printing a résumé, and a newcomer practicing English with a volunteer. None of them paid a cent. That is the quiet radicalism of a public library: it assumes everyone deserves access, and then it simply provides it.

What your card unlocks

Borrowing is just the beginning. Your card is the key to digital archives, language courses, museum passes, and a maker space stocked with 3D printers and sewing machines. Below are a few of the programs neighbors reach for most.

Hours & access

We are open seven days a week, and every floor is step-free. Large-print and audio collections live on the second floor, and assistive listening devices are available at the front desk.

Plan your visit

Riverside Community Library, 200 Maple Street. Buses 12 and 40 stop at the door. Parking and a bike corral are available on the Elm Street side. Questions? Ask any librarian — helping is literally our job.
