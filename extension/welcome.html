WebAble

The web, adapted to you.

Every site is built one way for everybody. WebAble changes it to suit the person reading it. Start by telling it what gets in your way. You will see the change on this page as you pick.

What would make the web easier for you?

Pick any that apply, or none. You can change all of it later, for every site or just one.

This page is a live preview. Nothing is saved until you choose.

Two shortcuts, the whole web.

Ctrl+Shift+K
The command bar: type what you need
On any site, try too bright, text 150%, read this to me, go to pricing, 2x speed, or search for gift cards.
Alt+Shift+H
Click anything without a mouse
Every link and button gets a short label. Type it to click. Hold Shift to open in a new tab. Voice control users can switch to numbers in Settings.
Ctrl+Shift+U
All 43 tools in one panel
Reader mode, dyslexia fonts, colour filters, read aloud, Site Check, and the rest.

One or more shortcuts were not set, because another extension or the browser already uses those keys.

Pin WebAble so it is one click away: open the puzzle-piece menu next to the address bar and press the pin beside WebAble.
