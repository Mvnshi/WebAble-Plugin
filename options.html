What WebAble doesn't do

  • ✕ We don't scrape or store the pages you visit.
  • ✕ We don't run telemetry. Zero. Not for "improving the product."
  • ✕ We don't override your screen reader, voice control, or magnifier.
  • ✕ We don't claim to fix websites. We adapt them locally for you.
  • ✕ We don't auto-enable tools on install. Every toggle is opt-in.
